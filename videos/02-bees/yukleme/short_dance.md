## Anlatım metni
Bees can tell each other exactly where to find food... by dancing. Inside the dark hive, a bee runs in a figure eight, and shakes her body in the middle. The angle of that waggle shows the direction to fly, compared to the Sun. And the longer she waggles, the farther away the flowers are. A scientist decoded this dance, and won a Nobel Prize for it.
