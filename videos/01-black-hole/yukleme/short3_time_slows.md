## Anlatım metni
If you fell into a black hole, your friends would never see you fall in. Near a black hole, time runs slower than far away. So from far away, you seem to fall slower... and slower... and slower. Your image turns redder, and dimmer, and slowly fades away. But for you, nothing slowed down. You crossed the edge in a moment. Both views are true. That's Einstein's relativity.
