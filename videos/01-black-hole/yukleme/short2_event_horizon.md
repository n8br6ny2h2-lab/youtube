## Anlatım metni
You could fall past the point of no return... and not even notice. Every black hole is surrounded by an invisible line called the event horizon. Cross it, and every possible path leads inward. Not even light can get back out. Near a giant black hole, like the one at the center of our galaxy, you'd feel nothing special as you cross. No wall. No bump.
