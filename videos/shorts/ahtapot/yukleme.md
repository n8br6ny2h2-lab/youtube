## Anlatım metni
An octopus has three hearts. Two of them pump blood through its gills. The third pumps it to the rest of the body. And its blood is blue, because it uses copper, not iron, to carry oxygen. Here's the strange part: when an octopus swims, that main heart stops beating. That's why octopuses would rather crawl. Oh, and about two-thirds of its brain cells are in its arms.
