## Anlatım metni
Why is the sky blue? Sunlight looks white, but it's actually a mix of every color of the rainbow. When it hits our air, tiny gas molecules scatter blue light much more than red. So blue light bounces all across the sky, from every direction. That's the blue you see. But at sunset, sunlight has to travel through much more air to reach you. Most of the blue gets scattered away on the journey... and the reds and oranges are what's left. Follow Kavci Lab for more.
