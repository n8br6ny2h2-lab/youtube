## Anlatım metni
This food can last for thousands of years. Archaeologists have found pots of honey in ancient Egyptian tombs, and it was still preserved. So why doesn't honey go bad? It has very little water, so bacteria dry out and can't grow. It's also acidic. And bees add an enzyme that slowly makes hydrogen peroxide, a natural germ killer. And here's the crazy part: one bee makes only about a twelfth of a teaspoon of honey in its whole life. Follow Kavci Lab for more.
