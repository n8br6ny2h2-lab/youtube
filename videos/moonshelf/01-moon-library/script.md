# Moonshelf TV · Story 1: The Moon's Night Library

- Kanal: Moonshelf TV (çocuklara özel, 6-10 yaş). Özgün hikâye, Kavci Games.
- Anlatıcı: ElevenLabs "Lily" (pFZP5JQG7iQjIQuC4Bku), yavaş ve sakin.
- Görüntü: Pixabay gerçek doğa çekimleri (ay, baykuş, tilki, ördek, balina) + kendi çizimlerimiz (ateş böcekleri, ışıklı kitap).
- Hikâyedeki hayvan bilgileri doğru: baykuş başını ~270° çevirebilir ve sessiz uçar (yumuşak, saçaklı tüyler); kızıl tilki karın altındaki fareyi duyabilir; ateş böceği ışığı neredeyse ısısızdır; kambur balinaların şarkısı suda çok uzağa gider; ördekler bir gözü açık uyuyabilir.

## Metin (paragraflar = sahneler)
P0: Hello, friend. Welcome to the Moonshelf. Get cozy, and take a slow breath in... and out. Tonight's book is called... The Moon's Night Library.
P1: High above the hills, when the sun goes to sleep, the Moon opens her library. It is a very special library. Its books are made of moonlight, and every book holds a little dream.
P2: Each night, the Moon looks down and whispers, "Who needs a dream tonight?"
P3: First, she finds Owl, sitting very still on a branch. Owl is wide awake. She can turn her head much farther than you can, almost all the way around! Her feathers are so soft that she can fly without making a sound. So the Moon gives Owl a quiet book, about gliding over sleeping fields.
P4: Next, the Moon sees Fox, trotting through the tall grass. Fox has big, clever ears. She can hear a tiny mouse, even under the snow. The Moon gives Fox a cozy book, about a warm den, and a soft, curled-up tail.
P5: Down in the meadow, little lights blink on, and off. Fireflies! They make their own light, a gentle glow that stays cool, not hot. They blink to say hello to each other. The Moon smiles, and gives them a book full of stars, so they can practice their twinkling.
P6: Far away, in the deep blue sea, a humpback whale is singing. Whale songs can travel a very long way through the water. The Moonlight shimmers on the waves, and the Moon sends down a book of ocean lullabies.
P7: By the quiet pond, the ducks are falling asleep. Some of them keep one eye open, just to keep watch. The Moon whispers, "Rest now, little ducks. I'll watch over you."
P8: At last, the Moon looks at you. Yes, you. She has saved one more book, just for you. What will your dream be about tonight? Maybe gliding like Owl... listening like Fox... glowing like a firefly... or singing like a whale.
P9: Close your eyes. Breathe in... and out. The Moon is keeping watch. And tomorrow night, the Moonshelf will open a brand new book. Goodnight, friend. Sweet dreams.
